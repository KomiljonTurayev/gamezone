GameZone UZ — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 April 2026 · Effective: 30 April 2026

1. Introduction

GameZone UZ ("the App") is a multi-game hub published by Turayev Komiljon ("we", "our", "us"), an independent developer based in Uzbekistan. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App. Contact: komiljon_torayev@epam.com

2. Age Requirement

GameZone UZ is intended for users aged 13 and older. The App is not directed at children under 13.

3. Information We Collect

The App itself does not require account registration and does not collect personal information directly. We do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Photos, contacts, or precise location
  • Voice or video recordings

The App stores game progress and settings locally on your device only.

4. Information Collected by Third Parties

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

Google AdMob (Advertising)

AdMob may collect device identifiers (Advertising ID), IP address, and approximate location to deliver relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization."

AdMob privacy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Google Play Services

Required for app delivery, integrity verification, and crash reporting. Privacy: policies.google.com/privacy

5. Permissions

The App requests:

  • Internet access — to load ads and game content
  • Network state — to detect connectivity

We do not request access to camera, microphone, contacts, location, or storage.

6. Children's Privacy

If we discover that a user under 13 has used the App, any associated data (which is minimal and stored locally) can be cleared by uninstalling the App. Parents can contact us to report concerns.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / Local Laws)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access information about data processed by third-party SDKs
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Object to processing
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

To exercise these rights: komiljon_torayev@epam.com

8. Data Security

Game data is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that store user data. Communications with ad networks use HTTPS encryption.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will reflect any changes. Material changes will be communicated via in-app notice.
